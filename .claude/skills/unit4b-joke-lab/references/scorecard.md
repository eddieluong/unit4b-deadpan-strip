# Joke scorecard (score 1–5 on each, total /40)

| # | Criterion | 5 means | 1 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Surprise** | the punchline reverses an assumption the audience didn't know they made | you can see it coming from the setup |
| 2 | **Clarity for a non-native viewer** | a 13-year-old reading captions in another country gets it in one pass | needs native wordplay, slang or local context |
| 3 | **Character fit** | only this character could say it (swap-test fails for everyone else) | any character could say it |
| 4 | **Brevity** | punchline ≤ 8 words, ends on the funny word | rambling, the laugh word is buried |
| 5 | **Visual payoff** | the reaction face or a silent image adds a second laugh | works only as audio |
| 6 | **Hook strength** | frame 1 + first line make you need the answer | slow start, no question raised |
| 7 | **Relatability** | most adults have felt this exact annoyance | niche experience |
| 8 | **Rewatch / share** | loops cleanly or has a quotable line people would comment | one-and-done |

Thresholds:
- **≥ 32**: write it.
- **26–31**: punch-up pass, then re-score.
- **< 26**: drop it.

Automatic zero (drop regardless of score): punches down at a real group, uses a real person/brand/tragedy,
shows violence instead of implying it, puts Penny in danger, repeats a premise or punchline from `episodes/BANK.md`.

When presenting scores to the user, show only the total and the one weakest criterion.
