# Finding topics people will laugh at

The best UNIT 4B topics are small, shared annoyances of adult life, ideally ones people are talking about
this month. Search wide, keep only what is universal and visual.

## Where to look (use WebSearch / WebFetch)
| Source | What it gives | Example queries |
|---|---|---|
| Relatable-humor communities | everyday pain points in people's own words | `site:reddit.com r/mildlyinfuriating rent`, `r/Adulting this week`, `worst roommate habits reddit` |
| Landlord / tenant talk | the show's home turf | `weirdest lease clauses`, `landlord excuses not to fix`, `security deposit horror stories` |
| Trend and meme trackers | formats and phrases going viral now | `viral meme format this week`, `trending TikTok sound comedy October` (use the current month and year) |
| Seasonal calendar | dependable hooks | upcoming holidays, daylight saving, back to school, tax season, heat waves, Black Friday |
| Tech annoyances | universal modern pain | `subscription price increase news`, `smart home fails`, `AI features nobody asked for` |
| Work life | relatable across countries | `return to office memes`, `email phrases passive aggressive`, `meeting that could have been an email` |
| Comedy shorts benchmarks | what already performs | if the vidIQ connector is available: outliers / trending shorts in "animated comedy", "relatable"; otherwise search `most viewed animated comedy shorts relatable` |

Rules for research:
- Prefer sources from the last 4 weeks for "current" topics; note the date.
- Extract the **pain point**, never the specific person, brand, or news story.
- Never use a real tragedy, a named public figure, a political issue, or a real company as the joke.
- Quote nothing long; paraphrase the shared feeling in one sentence.

## Evergreen bank (use when search is unavailable or to fill a batch)
Rent goes up · deposit never comes back · the "cozy" listing · thin walls · thermostat wars · roommate eats
your food · package stolen · Wi-Fi drops during a call · smart speaker misunderstands · subscription you
forgot · "per my last email" · the meeting that could have been an email · Monday · alarm clock · gym
resolution in January · plants you keep killing · the group chat · autocorrect · self-checkout · "unexpected
item in bagging area" · daylight saving · holiday relatives · birthday you forgot · diet starts tomorrow ·
the vet visit · the cat knocks things off tables · 3 a.m. thoughts · "quick question" emails · password rules ·
"your call is important to us".

## Turning a topic into a candidate
For each topic write one line in this shape:
`[pain point] → [which character makes it absurd] → [the format] → [the image in frame 1]`
Example: `Landlords never fix anything → Penny treats repairs as a premium subscription → anti-climax → Penny handing Steve a "Repair Plus" brochure in a flooded kitchen`.
