# Viral DNA: pilot research (2026-10-22)

Searches: stick figure comedy shorts · new apartment animated comedy · dad jokes animation · weird neighbors
animation · landlord animated comedy · roommate animated skit · moving into new apartment animation · ghost
roommate animation · haunted apartment comedy. 177 candidates → 22 picked → 15 transcripts (7 had captions
disabled). One transcript (usNwe2ljdec) was dropped from analysis because it is built on racial slurs.

## Patterns

- **Hook in the first second, every time.** The winners open on a line already mid-situation (a greeting at a
  door, "we have to talk", a question). None open on a logo, title card or establishing shot.
- **Character-intro shorts work for ghosts.** The first-person "this is my weird roommate" list (197k) and the
  cheap-haunted-apartment pitch (9.6k) both introduce a whole premise in under 30 s by listing odd habits, then
  end on calm acceptance. A list of 3–4 quick beats is the house format for a pilot.
- **Punchline lands at 75–90 %**, followed by a short tag (a reaction, a turn on the teller, or a deadpan
  "I'm used to it"). Median useful length: 30–70 s; the tightest ones are 24–38 s.
- **Mechanisms that repeat:** normalized absurdity (the horror is accepted as a household fact), status flip on
  the teller after a dad joke, money beating fear, and literal misunderstanding of a common word.
- **Dad-joke shorts live on the reaction, not the pun.** Rapid-fire pun lists get views but few comments; the
  ones with a reaction that turns on the teller get more engagement per view.
- **Titles:** "My roommate is a …", "POV: …", "When …", "This is my …": they name the situation.
