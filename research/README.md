# Research

`research/viral/<date>-<topic>/` holds what the joke lab learned from viral shorts:

- `candidates.md` / `candidates.json`: ranked videos (views, views/day, length, link)
- `DNA.md`: one card per video (hook, setup length, punch position, mechanism, why it spreads) and a Patterns summary
- `transcripts/`: downloaded subtitles, git-ignored. They are other creators' words, kept locally for analysis only.

Generate with `tools/viral_research.py` (see `.claude/skills/unit4b-joke-lab/references/viral-analysis.md`).
