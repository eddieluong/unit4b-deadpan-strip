## 2. The script table (write this, get the owner's OK, then produce)
One row per line. Columns:

| # | Speaker | VO or in-scene | Line (caption text) | Emotion | Intensity 1–5 | Stressed word | Pause after (beat type) | Picture (what the frame shows) | Motion (and its reason) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules for the table:
- **VO or in-scene** decides lip-sync. VO = the character tells it later (no lip movement on screen); in-scene = said in the moment (lip-sync, face matches the words).
- **Picture** is written for a viewer with no context: who, where, what changed. If it needs an explanation, redraw it.
- **Motion** only with a reason (new idea, reaction, punchline); otherwise "hold".
- **Rule check**: any custom, holiday, rule or unit the joke depends on is in a row before the joke.

## 3. Emotion arc (plot it before voicing)
Intensity 1 (flat) … 5 (explosion). The arc must rise; the stakes decide the height.
- Small annoyances (refreshing an app, a late delivery): 1–3, one short peak at most. [lesson 3]
- Real provocation at the climax (woken at 5:59 after an hour of trying; someone sits on your face): **one explosion, 5**,
  in the scene, loud, short (2–5 s), with a bleeped swear + grawlix. [lesson 14]
- After the explosion: contrast — the other character stays flat; the button is resigned (1). [Tập 2]
- Narration (VO) follows the arc too: cheerful → annoyed → sarcastic → through gritted teeth → whispering hope. [lesson 14]
- Never two explosions; never flat for the whole short. [lessons 3, 14]

Voice settings by intensity (ElevenLabs v3): 1–2 stability 0.5; 3–4 stability 0.3, style 0.6; 5 stability 0.0, style 0.8,
in its own take for the in-scene lines; narration at its natural speed (≈ 2 words/s), 0.25–0.3 s gaps; cut a beat to fit the length.

## 4. Swearing
Never real profanity (YouTube limits ads and reach for profanity in the first seconds and in titles; international audience).
Write the line up to the swear ("you little—"), add a 1 kHz bleep (0.6 s) and a grawlix bubble "#@$%&!" on screen.

## 5. Owner review points (to stop the fix-after-render loop)
1. **Script table + emotion arc** → owner OK (text only, minutes to change).
2. **Three key stills** (hook, climax, ending) → owner OK.
3. **Voice take** (listen once) → owner OK.
4. Full render → QA.md checklist → owner review. Changes at this stage should be small.

## 6. Production defaults that already work (don't re-decide)
- Format: voice-led, Steve first person (VO) + Snuggles deadpan; title line on top; kinetic word captions (stressed word in yellow).
- Timeline built from the voice take (`scene.js` LINE_DEFS + `buildTimeline()`); split by word timings (`split_words.py`).
- Bed seen from its foot; clocks as full-frame inserts; time tags; the cat planted in frame 1.
- Motion v2 (`motion.js`): on twos, 8 fps boil, eased poses, no overshoot; mouths only for in-scene lines.
- QA: `tools/qa_video.py` (punch = the climax line) + a contact sheet every 1 s before the owner sees it.
