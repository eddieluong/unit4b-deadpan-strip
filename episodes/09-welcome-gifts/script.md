**Title options** (≤ 50 characters, name the situation, not the joke)
1. My new neighbors brought welcome gifts
2. POV: you just moved into apartment 4B
3. Every neighbor brought a gift. That's the problem

**The joke in one sentence:** On moving day every neighbor brings Steve a gift (Nana: banana bread and a shovel,
Penny: a bill for the gift, Ted: a card from the old tenants, Snuggles: a dead mouse), and the cat tells us
everybody brings what they're good at, which makes the shovel in Steve's hand much worse.

Why it works as the first video: it introduces all five characters and their comic engines in 30 seconds.
Each gift is one character in a single visual. The Snuggles line ties all four gifts together, so the pilot
lands as one joke and not as a list.

**Notes for the animator**
- Stick cast (`cast_stick.js`). Every gift has to read in silhouette: the shovel twice Nana's height, a bow on the
  box and on the mouse, the card covered in scribbled signatures.
- Steve keeps the bread and the shovel in his arms for the rest of the short. The shovel is the setup.
- Ted enters through the closed door. His pink slippers come in a beat later, through the gap under the door.
- The doormat insert is the only shot without dialogue before the punchline: keep it still and silent.
- Running gag used: Steve being polite to everyone (he says thank you for every gift, with his face only).
