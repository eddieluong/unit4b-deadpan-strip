## 1. "One Percent"  (low battery)   score 33/40
- **Problem:** your phone is at 1 % and you must send one important message.
- **Why global (evergreen):** 72 % of urban Indian users feel anxious at <= 20 % battery
  ([Counterpoint](https://counterpointresearch.com/insight/survey-72-smartphone-users-india-experience-low-battery-anxiety));
  Americans start worrying at about 38 %
  ([Techspot/Talker](https://www.techspot.com/community/topics/survey-reveals-most-people-panic-when-their-phone-battery-drops-to-38.291753));
  Spain: 40 % have argued over a borrowed charger ([The Objective](https://theobjective.com/tecnologia/2026-04-29/cargamos-telefono-guia-bateria-sabes-carga/)).
  Demand proof: "Low Battery" cartoons with phones as characters have 8.1 M and 1.2 M views (different premise: ours is a human problem).
- **Frame 1 (0.0 s):** extreme close-up of Steve's sweaty face, eyes wide; phone held up, screen red "1%". Caption: `POV: your phone is at 1%`.
- **Beats (28 s):**
  - 0-2 s Steve whispers "One percent." Snuggles V.O.: "One percent. One message. One man." Hook = face + red 1 %.
  - 2-6 s Cable too short: Steve lies flat on the floor, arm stretched, socket on the far wall. Stays 1 %.
  - 6-10 s Socket is behind the fridge; he crawls under the table. Still 1 %. (silent beat, only the face)
  - 10-12 s Silent third: Steve in an absurd twist, phone clamped in teeth, cable in hand, cat watching.
  - ~13 s TURN: plugged in. Screen: "2%". Steve breathes out. Types "On my way". Bubble: "Sending..."
  - ~22 s PUNCH: pop-up "Battery low. 1% remaining." Phone dies. Steve (flat): "Thank you."
  - 23-25 s silent beat: black screen, his reflection.
  - 25-28 s Button: Steve pulls a power bank from his bag. Its light shows "1%". Snuggles: "It's a family business." Loop to frame 1.
- **Cast:** Steve, Snuggles (V.O.), cat body in frame. No lore needed.
- **Format:** escalation x3 with silent third + anti-climax (the warning costs the last percent) + loop.
- **Risks:** punch is a pop-up (screen text, needs a clear big caption); Ep 11/14 already use the cat; keep cat only a watcher.
  Weakest criterion: character fit (3), any sitcom could do it. Fix in punch-up: Snuggles' line carries the fit.

## 2. "Typing..."  (the three dots)   score 34/40
- **Problem:** you see the other person "typing..." for forever, then dots vanish, then a one-letter reply.
- **Why global (evergreen):** "three-dot anxiety" is documented across platforms incl. WhatsApp
  ([Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/i-hear-you/202506/why-you-cant-stop-thinking-about-that-text),
  [Digit India](https://www.digit.in/news/mobile-phones/waiting-for-message-replies-can-cause-anxiety-report-23845.html)); being left on read is the top pet peeve for 42 % in a UK
  survey ([Daily Brit](https://thedailybrit.co.uk/?p=7506)); 48 % of Indian singles worry about ghosting
  ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/lifestyle/from-should-i-text-to-what-did-that-mean-61-of-indian-singles-spend-hours-or-even-days-replaying-in-their-heads-1994445));
  72.7 % of US 18-21s lay awake over a missing reply (SellCell, Oct 2026). No dates/slang, works on Messenger/WhatsApp/Zalo-style apps (all show dots).
  Different from 15-ideas A (that was a planning chat).
- **Frame 1 (0.0 s):** a GIANT three-dot bubble floats in the room like a storm cloud; Steve and the cat stare up at it, mouths open.
  Caption: `POV: they're typing...`
- **Beats (28 s):**
  - 0-2 s Steve: "Are we still on for tonight?" Bubble: "Seen". Snuggles: "Seen. At 9:41."
  - 2-6 s Dots appear. Steve and cat lean in. Dots vanish. Steve's face drops.
  - 6-10 s Dots appear. Dots vanish. Sun to moon behind the window; the cat now has a tiny beard (time cheat).
  - 10-13 s Dots appear and STAY. Steve grips the table. Snuggles: "Someone is writing. Someone is writing a lot." Wall unrolls a paragraph imagined.
  - ~14 s TURN: ping! The reply bubble arrives, very small.
  - ~22 s PUNCH: the bubble says only "k". Steve: "...k?" Snuggles: "Forty minutes. One letter."
  - 23-25 s silent beat: Steve staring, dot-bubble shrinks to nothing.
  - 25-28 s Button: Steve types and deletes four times; sends "k". Cut to a silhouette across town: giant dots bloom over THEIR head. Loop.
- **Cast:** Steve, Snuggles; other texter is an unnamed silhouette. No lore.
- **Format:** escalation x3 (appear/vanish x2, then stay) + anti-climax + mirror loop.
- **Risks:** a screen joke, so the giant bubble must be a real animated object (done in frame 1). Surprise is modest because
  "k" is guessable; the mirror button is the second laugh. Weakest criterion: surprise (3).

## 3. "Two Minutes Away"  (food-delivery ETA)   score 33/40
- **Problem:** the app says the rider is "2 minutes away" for an hour.
- **Why global (evergreen):** UK Uber Eats reviews (Apr 2026) describe 1 h+ waits and repeated driver reassignment
  ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/uber-eats.co.uk?page=10)); India Domino's complaints in 2026
  ([Voxya](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/food-not-delivered/266459)); Philippines consumer group Filcore calls for tighter
  delivery-app rules ([Market Monitor](https://marketmonitor.com.ph/?p=55543)); HK Consumer Council: 60 % of 56 test orders missed the
  ETA ([The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/hong-kong-news/article/304781/Consumer-watchdog-reports-over-1000-complaints-against-food-delivery-platforms-last-year)).
  Delivery apps are huge in VN/PH, our core viewers. Not Ep 10 (that was a missed package at the door).
- **Frame 1 (0.0 s):** Steve at a laid table, fork and knife up, hollow stare; candle burned to a stub; phone propped on the plate
  showing "2 min". Caption: `POV: "2 minutes away"`.
- **Beats (29 s):**
  - 0-2 s Steve to the phone: "Two minutes." Snuggles: "That was an hour ago."
  - 2-6 s Phone: "2 min". Candle shorter. Steve ties a napkin round his neck.
  - 6-10 s Phone: "2 min". Steve now with stubble; a cobweb on the fork.
  - 10-13 s Silent third: phone "2 min", Steve asleep face-down in the bread basket.
  - ~14 s TURN: ping! "Your order has arrived." Steve explodes to the door.
  - ~22 s PUNCH: door opens on an empty hall. Across the hall, Nana at her door, chewing, holding his bag, smiling:
    "It's still warm, dear." Steve (flat): "...Enjoy."
  - 23-25 s silent beat: Nana waves with a drumstick; Steve and the empty fork.
  - 25-29 s Button: app pop-up "Rate your delivery." Steve taps five stars, smiling like a hostage. Loop to frame 1 (new order, "2 min").
- **Cast:** Steve, Snuggles (V.O.), Nana as a zero-lore sweet-face-dark gag (a neighbour who eats your food reads without lore).
- **Format:** escalation x3 + silent third + sweet face dark line + loop.
- **Risks:** punch at 76 % is fine, but the 5-star button is a second idea (cut if over 30 s). Nana line "still warm" is weak if food
  has been cold an hour: fine, it's the lie that sells it. Rider-as-villain avoided (real gig workers). Weakest: fit (4 but
  replaceable by any neighbour) and rewatch (3).
