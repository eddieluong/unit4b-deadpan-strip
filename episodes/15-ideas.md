## A. "I'll bring ice" — Thanksgiving group chat  (score 8.5/10)
- **Problem (one line):** you organise the family dinner chat and everyone volunteers for the easiest thing.
- **Timely:** Thanksgiving Nov 26, planning chats start early Nov; family-planning stress is a top-cited holiday stressor.
- **Frame 1 (0.0 s):** a dinner table covered in bags of ice, Steve holding one bread roll. Title: `POV: you made the Thanksgiving group chat`.
- **Beats:** Steve types "who brings what?" → 47 unread → Penny: "Ice." → Ted: "Ice." → Nana (silent third, just a 🧊 sticker) → turn ~14 s: "WHO is doing the turkey?" — chat goes "seen by 6", 3 days of silence (calendar flips) → Steve: "Fine. I'll do it."
- **Punch (~75 %):** Thursday 7 a.m. — turkey is a solid block; the thaw guide says "4 days". Steve stares.
- **Flat button:** Penny, deadpan: "Good news. We have ice." (Loop: back to the ice table.)
- **Format:** escalation ×3 with silent third + anti-climax. Zero lore needed.
- **Risk:** US-centric holiday; ice/frozen-turkey still reads internationally.

## B. "It's just allergies" — the sick person in the elevator  (score 8/10)
- **Problem:** someone is clearly sick and insists it's allergies, standing right next to you.
- **Timely:** flu rising in all 50 states (Oct 2026), early flu A share ~3× last year.
- **Frame 1:** Steve flattened against the elevator wall, cheeks puffed, holding his breath; a neighbour mid-sneeze. Title: `POV: they say it's "just allergies"`.
- **Beats:** cough — "allergies" → sneeze — "pollen" → thermometer beeps 39° — "it's November pollen" → every floor button is lit (elevator stops at all 12) → Steve's face goes purple, timer on screen.
- **Punch (~78 %):** doors open on the top floor; a ghost (Ted) is standing in the corner, who was in the elevator all along: "That's what I said. In 1987."
- **Flat button:** Steve exhales — straight into the neighbour's sneeze. Doors close.
- **Format:** escalation ×3 + dark one-liner. Ghost is readable as "a ghost" with zero lore.
- **Risk:** dark but not cruel (illness is mild, the death is 1987 and comedic).

## C. "Early Black Friday" that never ends  (score 7/10)
- **Problem:** "early deals" start Nov 5 and you never know when the real lowest price is.
- **Timely:** Target early Black Friday Nov 5–7, Walmart Nov 12–15, Black Friday Nov 27.
- **Frame 1:** Steve asleep at his laptop, 40 price tabs, one TV in the cart for 41 days. Title: `POV: you're waiting for the Black Friday price`.
- **Beats:** banners escalate — "Early Black Friday" → "Black Friday Preview" → "Pre-Early Access (members only)" → Penny as the shop bot: "Membership: 49.99."
- **Punch (~80 %):** Black Friday arrives; the price is $10 higher than Nov 5.
- **Flat button:** Nana, who bought it at full price in September, watching it: "Lovely picture, dear."
- **Format:** escalation + anti-climax. Universal FOMO.
- **Risk:** mostly screens/text → harder to make visual, weaker motion per line.
