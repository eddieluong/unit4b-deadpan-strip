**Title options:** "The self-checkout thinks I'm a criminal" · "Unexpected item: me" · "POV: you buy one banana"

**The joke in one sentence:** Steve buys one banana, the self-checkout keeps flagging it, the light flashes, a bored
teenager comes over and says exactly what Steve already told the machine — and next week the machine flags him before
he scans anything.
