## 1. Spam calls — "Unknown Number"
- **Problem:** an unknown number rings; you know it is spam, but it might be the one real call, so you answer anyway.
- **Sources:**
  - Philippines: Hiya Q1 2025: 55 % of unknown calls were spam (3rd in APAC); Whoscall: scam calls +78 % in Q3 2025 (62,390) — https://mb.com.ph/2025/10/14/scam-calls-in-philippines-surge-78-in-q3-2025-anti-scam-app-report ; https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/scitech/technology/973827/sms-scams-drop-86-6-in-2025-scammers-shift-to-making-calls-whoscall-report/story
  - India: LocalCircles (Feb 2024): 6 in 10 Indians get 3+ spam calls a day — https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/top-story/story/kya-aapko-loan-chahiye-6-in-10-indians-get-3-or-more-pesky-calls-each-day-reveals-survey-418013-2024-02-19
  - UK/US: Hiya: ~28-30 % of UK unknown calls are spam, 13 % fraud; US 11 spam calls/month vs UK 4 — https://blog.hiya.com/global-call-threat-report
  - Vietnam: only old Truecaller data (14.7 spam calls/user/month, 2020) — https://truecaller-web-main.pages.dev/blog/insights/truecaller-insights-top-20-countries-affected-by-spam-calls-in-2020-2
- **Evergreen/trending:** evergreen; PH scam calls had a dated 2025 surge (mild trend, 2025 not Nov 2026). VN data stale.
- **Visual:** frame 1 = Steve frozen, huge ringing phone "UNKNOWN" with sweat drops on it, Snuggles V.O. Strong face + one object. Keep it nuisance telemarketing, NOT fraud victims (tragedy risk).

## 2. One Percent — low battery (IDEAS backup #1)
- **Problem:** 1 % battery and one message that must go out.
- **Sources:**
  - US: Talker Research: average worry starts at 38 % — https://www.techspot.com/community/topics/survey-reveals-most-people-panic-when-their-phone-battery-drops-to-38.291753 ; LG: 9 in 10 "panic" under 20 % — https://www.bustle.com/articles/162358-what-is-low-battery-anxiety-it-affects-a-surprisingly-huge-amount-of-people-survey-shows
  - India: Counterpoint: 72 % of urban users anxious at <=20 % — https://counterpointresearch.com/insight/survey-72-smartphone-users-india-experience-low-battery-anxiety
  - Spain/UK: 40 % argued over a charger (The Objective, Apr 2026) — https://theobjective.com/tecnologia/2026-04-29/cargamos-telefono-guia-bateria-sabes-carga/ ; Vodafone 28 % worry point is UNVERIFIED (secondary blog only).
  - PH/VN: NO survey found. Only retail notes that PH brownouts drive power-bank demand and an old Nielsen item ranking battery life high for Filipinos (rcrwireless.com/?p=31365, undated). Weak.
- **Evergreen.** Visual: Steve's face, phone red "1%", cable too short. Very easy to frame; character fit weak (any sitcom).

## 3. Two Minutes Away — delivery ETA (IDEAS backup #2)
- **Problem:** app says "2 min" for an hour.
- **Sources:**
  - Worldwide: Statista 2022: long delivery time is the top delivery frustration (>1/3) — https://statista.com/statistics/1366234/online-food-delivery-top-frustrations-worldwide
  - Philippines: delivery delays a top e-commerce concern (FedEx survey, Sep 2025; parcels not food) — https://malaya.com.ph/business-news/e-commerce-players-hit-2m-delivery-issues-top-concern ; PH Reddit complaints of identical ETAs (Dec 2025) — https://interaksyon.philstar.com/trends-spotlights/2025/12/02/305820/same-day-delivery-app-slow-progress-orders/
  - SE Asia/Vietnam: Parcel Perform: >90 % of complaints from 80,000 consumers were late delivery/no status; Vietnam 2nd-highest one-star share (parcels) — https://www.parcelperform.com/insights/delivery-the-biggest-pain-point-in-e-commerce-in-southeast-asia ; VN food delivery is huge ($2.1 bn 2025, ShopeeFood/GrabFood 48 % each) but no VN lateness data — https://theinvestor.vn/vietnams-online-food-delivery-market-tops-21-bln-as-foreign-apps-dominate-d18259.html
  - Also from IDEAS (not re-checked): HK Consumer Council 60 % of 56 test orders missed ETA; UK Uber Eats Trustpilot.
- **Evergreen.** Strong reach for VN/PH core audience. Visual: laid table, candle stub, phone "2 min". Avoid blaming riders (real gig workers).

## 4. "Five More Minutes" — snooze / alarm
- **Problem:** alarm goes off, you bargain with it, you are late anyway.
- **Sources:**
  - Global: Mass General Brigham (Sci. Reports, 2025): 56 % of 3 M sleep sessions ended with snooze; US/Sweden/Germany highest, Japan/Australia lowest; no PH/VN breakdown — https://www.today.com/today/amp/rcna206805
  - India: Times of India (2022) on Notre Dame study: 57 % habitual snoozers — https://telanganatoday.com/study-finds-people-hit-the-snooze-button-on-alarm-more-often-than-thought
  - UK: Premier Inn poll of 2,000 Brits (~1/4 snooze once) and Withings ~20,000 users: ~half snooze — https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-many-times-do-most-people-hit-the-snooze-button-and-more-fascinating-alarm-clock-stats-243011
- **Evergreen.** Good global fit, no vocabulary. Visual: Steve and the phone alarm in a negotiation, 7 alarms stacked. Risk: common sitcom trope, low surprise; BANK has DST/3 A.M. sleep-adjacent jokes.

## 5. Family group chat "Good Morning" spam
- **Problem:** relatives post glittery good-morning images and forwards every day; you can't leave the group.
- **Sources:**
  - India: Western Digital survey (2018): 1 in 3 Indian smartphone users run out of storage daily, blamed on good-morning images — https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/good-morning-messages-eating-up-smartphone-space-in-india-report-532992
  - Malaysia/SE Asia: "5 annoying relatives in every WhatsApp family chat" (FMT, 2022) — https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/leisure/2022/08/21/5-annoying-relatives-found-in-every-whatsapp-family-chat-group
  - Vietnam: Zalo is the top app for family/friends in all generations (Decision Lab Q4 2023 via Statista) — https://www.Statista.Com/statistics/1229647/vietnam-leading-social-media-platforms-to-connect-with-friends-and-family-by-generation/ ; US: 42 % say group messaging "feels like a part-time job" — https://www.securedatarecovery.com/blog/group-messaging-feels-like-job
  - Philippines: no source found.
- **Evergreen, dated evidence (2018-2023).** Visual: strong (glitter rose GIF avalanche, Nana as sender only as a zero-lore gag). Overlaps IDEAS "group-chat who brings what" (15-ideas A) only loosely; still check.

- No PH/IN/VN data found; "forgotten Wi-Fi password" stats found were unsourced SEO pages: do NOT cite. Evergreen, one-country evidence. Visual OK (Steve holding the router over his head), weak otherwise.
