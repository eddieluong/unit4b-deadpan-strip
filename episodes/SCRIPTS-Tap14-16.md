## Tập 14 — Every Floor  (22/11)
**Real problem:** you're late, and the lift stops at every floor with nobody there.

**Title line:** `POV: you're late and the lift stops at every floor`

**Frame 1:**
- A giant lift button panel (≥ 40 %), with EVERY button lit, 12 down to G.
- Steve's sweaty face beside it (≥ 25 %), in a tie, holding a coffee.

**Mechanism:**
1. Steve leans back into the corner. His huge backpack covers the bottom of the panel. This is the plant, in plain sight, and it pays off on rewatch.
2. Two empty stops.
3. A fast montage of decay.
4. One peak at the CLOSE button.
5. He shifts, and sees his bag pressing every button.
6. The doors open at G on a waiting crowd.

**Punch shape (new):** the cause reveal (self-inflicted), then a stranger's one full reframing line.

## Tập 16 — One Bulb  (24/11)
**Real problem:** you untangle the holiday lights for an hour, and they don't light.

**Title line:** `POV: getting the Christmas lights out`

**Frame 1:**
- A giant knot ball of lights (≥ 40 %).
- Steve's face peeking out of it (≥ 25 %), the plug in his hand.

**Mechanism:**
1. Plant: "I put them away NEATLY."
2. Untangling gags.
3. Plugged in: dark.
4. A bulb-by-bulb test montage.
5. One peak: all lit.
6. Escalation: he plugs in EVERYTHING; the whole house is covered.
7. Then the whole street goes dark.

**Punch shape (new):** a big visual consequence, then one full ironic muttered line in the dark.
